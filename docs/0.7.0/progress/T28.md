## What landed

- **Shipped bake stays product-owned.** `StudioEnvironmentVariant::ProductProcedural`
  is bake version 2 and remains the only automatically selected variant.
  `NeutralReference` exists solely for the measured T28 comparison and is never
  selected by the product.
- **Explicit direct/IBL contract.** The camera-relative key/fill/rim directions
  are `(0.45, -0.55, 0.70)`, `(-0.70, 0.25, 0.38)` and
  `(0.18, 0.76, 0.28)`; base intensities are 1.30/0.38/0.24. The CPU multiplies
  the preset/user scales and the shader has no second literal intensity source.
  Environment intensity continues to scale the direct rig and IBL together.
- **Contribution-based presentation.** `MaterialSummary` now carries contributed
  triangle counts and unlit/textured/transparent shares. A majority of the
  contributed surface must be alpha/transmissive before the checker is chosen;
  a dense mesh with no material catalog becomes Scan/Sculpt, while an authored
  untextured PBR mesh stays Studio PBR regardless of triangle count.
- **Environment provenance and cost.** Debug test-control fields expose bake
  version, variant, identity hash, specular sample count, resident bytes,
  upload bytes and bake microseconds. They are read-only and add no shipping
  authority.
- **No per-model tuning.** The lane and renderer contain no asset-name or
  material-name exception.
- **External review framing.** The Car and Camera external views now frame the
  complete product (`distanceScale` 1.0) instead of a cropped close-up, and the
  Suit entry uses the corpus's real `Advanced Crew Escape Suit.glb` name. All
  three pass the usefulness/centering check before the region samples run.
- **No stale source-string assumption.** The T26 opaque target writes
  `opacity`; the T28 tonemap assertion was updated to that contract instead of
  the obsolete authored `base.a`.

## Reusable facts for T29/T30

- **T26 opacity vs T28 assertion.** The opaque pixel shader writes
  `output.color = float4(max(color,0.0f), opacity)`; `base.a` remains authored
  coverage. A stale `base.a` source-string assertion in
  `PresentationToneMapTests.cpp` was the one failing check in the timed-out T28
  run. Do not reintroduce a display-space or base-alpha assumption into the
  resolve contract.
- **Lane execution.** Run the front-matter `verify.py` command or `--fast`; do
  not call `studio_lighting.run(..., fast=False)` from a silent one-liner.
  Redirect the gate to a log and watch `diagnostics/progress.log`. The viewer
  watchdog kills `Preview3D` after 300 s without a heartbeat
  (`--stall-seconds`), so a driver or `PrintWindow` hang fails the lane instead
  of consuming the task timeout.
- **Environment expectations.** `NeutralReference` is a comparison bake, not a
  shipping option. The procedural bake still mixes warm/cool panel separation;
  the neutral reference and the measured summary are the evidence for that
  limitation, not a hidden dependency.
- **Selection fallback.** Legacy callers that cannot supply contribution leave
  the contributed counts zero and the selector falls back to the bounded record
  counts. New callers should supply per-material triangle counts and map masks
  so the visible-surface rule is active.

## Remaining limitations

- The shipping environment is a product-owned procedural bake, not a licensed
  studio HDRI. The T28 measured comparison chose the procedural bake; an
  external HDR bake remains future work.
- The procedural panels still show warm/cool separation. This is recorded as a
  limitation in the review record and assigned to T28/T30, not hidden by the
  aggregate image checks.
