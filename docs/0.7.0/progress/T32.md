## What landed

- **Pure module.** `interactive-viewer/src/render/ScreenSpaceAo.{h,cpp}` owns the
  per-mode strength/radius calibration, the bounded radius conversion, the
  indirect-only composite helper, authored/screen OC combination and the target
  budget. `Tests.Unit.exe "[screen-space-ao]"` pins 67 assertions, including a
  no-GPU compile of the raw/blur shaders and the `AO_INDIRECT` material variant.
- **Indirect MRT.** The opaque material pass has an `AO_INDIRECT` PS variant that
  writes scene colour + the environment/IBL/ambient share + pick id. The
  historical two-target layout remains as `*Legacy*` PSOs for the AO-off
  fallback. Compact and position-only CAD geometry have AO variants too.
- **AO passes.** `D3D12ViewerPath::RenderScreenSpaceAo` reconstructs
  camera-target-relative positions from the existing `D32_FLOAT` depth with the
  inverse view-projection, builds normals from the four neighbour positions, and
  runs a fixed 12-sample interleaved-gradient spiral. A sample contributes via
  the surface horizon or by being closer to the eye (narrow-gap/wall junction).
  A second pass applies a 5x5 depth-edge-aware bilateral filter.
- **Indirect-only resolve.** The resolve subtracts
  `indirect * (1 - aoMask)` from the linear HDR scene *before* the OIT composite,
  exposure, tone map and sRGB transfer. Direct light, emission, glass
  reflection/transmission and the display transform are never multiplied by the
  mask. Resolve diagnostics 5/6/7 expose the AO mask, the indirect share and the
  occluded indirect share.
- **Transparent/masked policy.** AO runs after opaque/Clay-overlay geometry and
  before the transmission snapshot, OIT and pick passes. BLEND/transmissive
  surfaces do not write opaque depth or the indirect target; MASK holes are
  authored discard. A window, cutout hole or background cannot receive an
  opaque AO sheet.
- **Passive controls.** `A`, the More menu item and the persisted
  `ambientOcclusionEnabled` preference toggle AO. Per-mode defaults: Studio
  0.60/0.10, Clay 0.85/0.14, Directional 0.55/0.09 (strength/scene-radius
  fraction). Debug fields 287-299 (inside the existing Debug guard) expose the
  toggle, bounded overrides, target bytes, CPU/GPU pass time, fallback reason
  and raw reconstruction/normal/gap/radius inspection views.
- **Lifecycle.** The indirect HDR target and two R8 masks are viewport-sized,
  accounted in `AccountedAllocationBytes`, rebuilt on resize and device
  recovery, and derived from the current drawable set every frame. A 2-query
  GPU timestamp heap + 16-byte readback records the AO pass without a
  same-frame wait (field 299). Allocation failure/forced/unsupported/diagnostic
  /point-line-only content records an AO-off fallback and draws normally.
- **Fixture/lane.** `ao-probe` and `ao-probe-large` in `fixtures.py`;
  `tests/render-quality/screen_space_ao.py`, `profiles/T32.json`, and
  `tests/render-quality/review/T32-review.json`. `verify.py` routes T32 and
  includes it in the unit-test build tuple. `viewer.apply_controlled_presentation`
  now explicitly enables AO and resets the debug mask view.

## Reusable facts / gotchas

- **Target size vs viewport rect.** The AO shader maps `SV_POSITION` to
  full-target UV and texels with the swap-chain extent. Passing the smaller 3D
  viewport rect made every neighbour sample land far away (the first symptom was
  an almost-white mask with only edge lines).
- **Camera-target-relative frame.** Positions are relative to the camera
  *target*, not the eye. Orient reconstructed normals toward
  `gAoEye = gEyeSelection.xyz`, never the origin.
- **Wall-to-wall corners.** A pure horizon test (`dot(n, delta) > bias`) misses
  perpendicular walls; the extra closer-to-eye/closing term is what makes
  narrow gaps and the inside of a box read. Without it the mask was restrained
  to the point of being invisible.
- **Mask diagnostics.** Resolve diag 5/6/7 and AO debug views 1-9 are Debug-only.
  Use `v.capture()` rather than `wait_stable()` for a mostly-white AO mask;
  `wait_stable` rejects it as blank. Reset field 253 to 0 before normal pairs.
- **Field reads vs writes.** Fields 287/288/289/294 interpret lParam as a write.
  Read them with `send_field(hwnd, field, -1)`. Sending 0 silently disables AO
  and persists it.
- **Forced fallback test.** `aoForceFallback` means AO-off; compare it to the
  ordinary AO-disabled image (287=0), not to the restored AO-on image.
- **GPU timing.** The query heap writes into a READBACK buffer resolved in the
  same command list; consume it one frame later via the slot fence. Do not map
  it in the recording frame.
- **Fixture.** `ao-probe-large` is the same assembly through a root node scale
  of 8. The glTF `_mesh_builder` placeholder bounds are patched after `finish()`.
- **Allocation failure.** Set `PREVIEW3D_TEST_AO_ALLOCATION_FAILURE=1` in the
  viewer launch environment; the lane runs this in a separate process.
