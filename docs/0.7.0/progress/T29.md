## Reusable facts

- **Effective bucket location.** `D3D12ViewerPath::RenderFrame` defines
  `flattenedForClay`, `isBlendTriangle`, `isForwardTransmission`,
  `isClayOverridden` and `drawRank`. Clay triangles are rank 0 (solid prepass),
  Clay-overridden authored BLEND/transmission are rank 1, transparent rank 2.
  `firstClayOverlay` and `firstTransparent` split the list; `opaqueEnd` is the
  smaller of the two so non-Clay modes still stop before transparent draws.
- **Overlay PSOs.** `texturedClayPipelineState`,
  `texturedClayMirroredPipelineState`,
  `texturedClayDoubleSidedPipelineState` and `clayPipelineState` are created
  with `DEPTH_FUNC_LESS_EQUAL`, depth writes on and
  `DepthBias=-1000, SlopeScaledDepthBias=-1.0, DepthBiasClamp=0`. Do not reuse
  them for normal Studio/Directional geometry.
- **Shader.** `PSMain` treats `clay` as `gLighting.x in (0.5,1.5)`. It samples
  base-color alpha only when `(flags&4)` (MASK) for the cutoff; `effectiveCoverage`
  is 1 in Clay. `f0/f90` are zero in Clay and `specularEnvironmentScale` is 0.
  Studio key/fill/rim scales are multiplied by 0.72/1.18/0.55 in Clay.
- **Debug fields.** 143/144/145 are effective bucket/depth/cull; 274 authored
  bucket, 275 authored depth write, 276 Clay override active. The WM_APP+104
  guard allows `wParam <= 276`. `diagnostics.material_records` reads all six.
  `RefreshDebugMaterials` uses `debugLightingMode_`; `RenderOneFrame` refreshes
  once when the overlay's lighting mode changes.
- **Lane files.** `tests/render-quality/clay_mode.py`,
  `profiles/T29.json`, fixture ids `clay-review`, `line-point-obj`,
  `step-assembly`. `verify.py` imports `clay_mode` and routes T29 after T28;
  T29 is also in the unit-test build tuple.
- **Fixture gotchas.**
  - `_mesh_builder` writes placeholder `POSITION min/max = [-1,1]`. `clay_review`
    patches real bounds after `finish()`; automatic framing uses them.
  - glTF LINES primitives are currently rejected by the bounded section
    validator (`ImportProtocolViolation`, ValidateSection) regardless of vertex
    count. Use the OBJ line/point fixture for mixed topology. OBJ `l`/`p` are
    only emitted by the adapter fallback when there are no triangles, so the
    fixture is line/point-only.
  - The 241 MB external Voron STEP fails `StepHostLimit` after ~302 s in Debug.
    Use `tests/fixtures/stp-spike/assembly_ap214.stp` for a small STEP assembly
    (loads in ~7 s).
- **Car speckle.** The white speckles along Car windshield/roof/window edges
  appear in Studio too. They are pre-existing (`car-authored-window-speckle`),
  not a Clay blend seam; do not attribute them to this override. Clay does
  remove colored glass banding and interior leakage.
- **Pick.** Use field 255 with normalized coordinates for a robust pick in the
  lane; `pick_image` and physical field 258 are DPI-sensitive. Capture the
  round-trip images before picking so a selection outline cannot change the
  MAE.
- **Round-trip comparison.** Compare import fields excluding
  `presentedFrames`, `detailRequests` and `requestedDetails`; they advance
  during the session. Texture counters, vertices/triangles/materials/gpuBytes,
  camera, exposure and pick id must be identical.
